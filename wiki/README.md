# wiki/ — synthesis

This folder holds what you make of your raw material: summaries, patterns, the compiled picture of a client, a topic, or a problem, drawn together from several captures in `raw/`.

Two things make wiki different from the rest of your brain:

1. **Wiki can be rewritten from scratch.** A wiki file is a snapshot of your current thinking. When your understanding shifts, regenerate it rather than patching it. That is the point of keeping synthesis separate from source.
2. **Every wiki file lists its sources.** At the bottom, link the `raw/` files it draws from. That way you, or your AI employee, know what a fresh version needs to account for when you rebuild it.

The short version: `raw/` is what happened. `wiki/` is what you make of it. Raw never changes; wiki always can.
